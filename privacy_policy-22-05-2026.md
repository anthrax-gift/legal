We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes take effect immediately upon posting. Please review this Privacy Policy regularly for updates.

## Retention

• Account registration data (username, invite key metadata) and service logs are retained only as necessary for operation, security, and legal compliance.  
• Specific retention periods may vary by data type; contact us to request details or earlier deletion.

## Data Disclosure
We do not share, sell, or provide any personal information or technical data about our users to third parties, except when required by law. Your information is used only to operate and improve the Service.

## Manage Your Info
You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time. To request deletion, email cupid_services@proton.me. We will process requests promptly and remove your data from our systems.

## Children's Privacy
The Service is intended for users aged 13 or older, or the minimum age of digital consent in your jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under that age. If we learn we have collected a child’s personal information without verified parental consent, we will delete it promptly. If you believe we have collected a child’s information, contact us immediately at cupid_services@proton.me.
